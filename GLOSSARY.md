# Glossary

**Source**: A third-party directory we read brand data from. There are three: Leaping Bunny (US/Canada directory, the main one), Cruelty Free International (the global directory for the same certification), and Cruelty-Free Kitty.

**Leaping Bunny certified**: The brand appears in the Leaping Bunny directory or in the Cruelty Free International approved-brands directory. Appearing in either one is enough.

**Source entry**: What one Source says about one brand, as it was published: its status, date and link. The Cruelty Free International date is the listing's publication date on its website, not an approval date, and its own listing pages don't show it, so the site doesn't display it.

**Verdict**: The single conclusion the site shows for a brand, derived from the Source entries. It is one of:
- **Certified cruelty-free** (*Libre de crueldad, certificada*): Leaping Bunny certified, whatever Cruelty-Free Kitty says.
- **Verified cruelty-free** (*Libre de crueldad, verificada por CFK*): not Leaping Bunny certified; Cruelty-Free Kitty says cruelty-free.
- **Not cruelty-free** (*No es libre de crueldad*): not Leaping Bunny certified; Cruelty-Free Kitty says not cruelty-free.
- **Grey area** (*Zona gris*): not Leaping Bunny certified; Cruelty-Free Kitty says grey area.
- **Unknown** (*Sin datos*): in no Source.

**Note**: Extra information shown next to a Verdict that never changes it. Examples: a parent company that tests on animals or isn't certified, sales in mainland China, or a disagreement between Sources.

**Grey-area reason**: Why Cruelty-Free Kitty places a brand in the grey area: the testing questions it answered "Uncertain" (finished products, ingredients, suppliers, third parties, sold where required by law).

**Alias**: A manual rule that says two differently named Source entries are the same brand, or that one Source entry should be ignored.
